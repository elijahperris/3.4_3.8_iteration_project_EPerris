Wellington's Walks

Porirua and Tawa

Te Ngahere O Tawa

Te Ngahere-o-Tawa is a beautiful bush walk located in the hills above Tawa, offering a peaceful escape into nature with stunning views across the surrounding suburbs and coastline. The track winds through a mix of native bush and pine forest, creating a calm and refreshing environment filled with birdsong and fresh air. You should do this walk because it is a great way to relax, improve your fitness, and enjoy the outdoors without needing to travel far from the city. The gentle climb and scenic surroundings make it suitable for both casual walkers and people looking for a more active adventure.

Redwood Bush

Redwood Bush is a scenic loop walk that takes you through one of Wellington’s treasured native forest reserves. The trail is surrounded by tall trees, lush greenery, and native wildlife, giving walkers a chance to experience the beauty of New Zealand’s natural environment up close. You should do this walk because it is peaceful, easy to follow, and perfect for clearing your mind while getting some exercise. The shaded tracks and quiet atmosphere make it an ideal place for families, nature lovers, and anyone wanting a break from busy everyday life.

Mount Rangituhi

The Trelissick Park Loop is a scenic bush walk that follows winding tracks through regenerating native forest alongside the Kaiwharawhara Stream. The loop combines peaceful greenery, birdlife, and gentle stream crossings, making it feel surprisingly wild despite being close to the city. The walk is especially popular with locals because of its relaxed atmosphere and variety of tracks that can suit both casual walkers and regular runners. It’s an excellent choice for anyone looking for a quieter nature walk where they can enjoy birdsong, shaded trails, and a calm escape from busy urban life.
