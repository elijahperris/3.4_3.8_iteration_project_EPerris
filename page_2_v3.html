Wellington's Walks

Central Wellington

Skyline Walkway

The Skyline Walkway in Wellington is one of the city's most scenic and adventurous routes, running across the hills and offering fantastic panoramic views of the harbour, the coastline and the surrounding countryside. It is popular with hikers, runners and mountain bikers due to the open scenery and atmosphere. As you walk along the path you can enjoy the fresh air, the rolling hills and the native plants while at the same time getting a good workout from the steep parts. It is a pleasant way of experiencing Wellington's natural beauty and is particularly rewarding on clear days when the views extend for miles. A major advantage of the Skyline Walkway is the fact that its open landscape gives the walk a much more remote and peaceful feeling than many other paths near the city. The change in altitude also makes it an ideal option for those who would like to challenge their fitness without losing the beautiful scenery. Another notable feature is the variety of views, since from different points along the track both the city and the more natural countryside can be seen. The exposed hills also give a strong feeling of adventure, particularly when the wind is blowing over the ridgelines. Since it combines exercise, beautiful scenery and adventure, the Skyline Walkway is an excellent choice for anyone looking for a more demanding walk in Wellington.

Southern Walkway

The Southern Walkway is a popular walking route in Wellington which leads walkers through a combination of city streets, parks, forest regions, and hilltops. It links up various sections of the city and provides excellent views of the harbour, Cook Strait, and the neighbouring suburbs. A great number of people take this walk since it combines physical activity with sightseeing, thereby making it both relaxing and exciting. The Southern Walkway is also an excellent opportunity to discover Wellington's history and environment, with numerous spots where one can stop, rest and enjoy the views during the walk. One of the main advantages of the Southern Walkway is the variety it offers, since the scenery changes along the route rather than remaining constant throughout. This means that it is appealing to those who like to discover different parts of Wellington in a single trip. The fact that the walk includes urban areas, green spaces, forests and hills also illustrates the close connection between Wellington's natural environment and the city. The route is especially rewarding for individuals who enjoy sightseeing as there are a number of different vantage points en route. Because it combines nature, history, exercise and city scenery, the Southern Walkway provides a unique experience when compared to more isolated walking tracks.

Red Rocks Walk

The Red Rocks Walk is situated on the rugged southern coast of Wellington and is well known for its spectacular coastal views and its unusual red rocks. It is a favourite among both local people and tourists since it provides magnificent views of the ocean, fresh sea air and the opportunity to see seals lying by the shore at particular times of the year. The broad gravel trail makes the walk suitable for a wide range of fitness levels, and the sound of the crashing waves together with the rocky terrain give an exciting atmosphere. Hiking on the Red Rocks trail is at the same time relaxing and invigorating, allowing visitors to get in touch with nature and to enjoy one of Wellington's most attractive coastal regions. A major advantage of the Red Rocks Walk is its special coastal setting, which gives it a very different character from the hill and forest paths found in Wellington. The steep cliffs, the rocks, the ocean and the strong coastal winds produce a thrilling feeling of being near the wild side of nature. The possibility of spotting seals is also a special feature that makes the walk memorable and offers visitors the chance to watch local wildlife in its natural habitat. Because the track is relatively wide, walkers do not have to worry about navigating a narrow or difficult path and can therefore concentrate on appreciating the views. In summary, the Red Rocks Walk is especially good for those who are looking for a relaxing coastal experience along with dramatic scenery, wildlife and a sense of adventure.
