Johnsonville and Kandallah

Mount Kaukau

The Mount Kaukau walk is a very popular hiking trail due to its beautidul views and accessibility. This walk takes hikers through an open hillside and well-established paths up to the summit overlooking the transmission tower. From the top, there are fantastic views of Wellington Harbour, the Hutt Valley, and Porirua and, on a good day, the sights of the South Island. The Mount Kaukau walk is great for those who want to get some exercise and enjoy magnificent views from the top. This trail is perfect for fulfilling your need for adventure and an adrenalin rush while giving you a sense of accomplishment when you conquer it. This trail is ideal for hikers seeking breathtaking views since there is an incredible variety that increases throughout the walk up the mountain. It is also an advantage that the trail is on an open hillside offering sensational panoramic views with no obstruction from the hikers, unlike in many other hiking trails. In addition to great views, walkers also get an excellent workout. They might find that walking up Mount Kaukau is a challenging task, and the reward at the top is an even greater satisfaction when they see the amazing views from the summit.Mount Kaukau trail is perfect for hikers who have the desire to experience an active adventure and enjoy great from the top.

Otari Wilton Bush Reserve

Ōtari-Wilton’s Bush is a tranquil native forest reserve and New Zealand’s only public botanic garden, offering a range of walking tracks from easy short walks to more challenging tramps through the forest of giant rimu trees, ferns, and scenic waterfalls. The walk also includes a canopy walkway and birdwatching opportunities with chances to spot native species such as tūī, kererū, and kākā. This is the perfect place to unwind and enjoy nature’s finest. Ōtari-Wilton’s Bush is a great option for a walk or a more challenging bushtrack for those who want to explore Wellington’s wilderness and learn about and see New Zealand’s native flora and fauna. The advantage of Ōtari-Wilton’s Bush is that it is a native valley offering fantastic biodiversity and showcasing New Zealand’s natural beauty, so hikers can enjoy a peaceful walk through a forest abundant with native wildlife. Being in a large forest also means that many parts are shady, providing cool walks for hikers who want to get some exercise. Ōtari-Wilton’s Bush has a variety of tracks to offer something for everyone, including shorter and easier tracks for hikers who want a less tiring walk. The opportunity to birdwatch is also an added bonus, as well as many other attractions that Ōtari-Wilton’s Bush has to offer. The focus on nature and conservation also makes Ōtari-Wilton’s Bush an excellent choice for people who appreciate a walk in the forest that has educational value.

Trelissick Park loop

The Trelissick Park Loop is a scenic bush walk, featuring shaded walks through beautiful bush with lots of birdlife and scenic walks over wetlands along Kaiwharawhara Stream. The trail is great for local walks and is popular with locals and visitors due to the excellent mix of short and more challenging options and the opportunity to enjoy a variety of landscapes while getting away from the stresses of the city. It is the perfect option for nature lovers seeking a short walk in the forest with beautiful scenery and an abundance of native birdlife. One of the best things about the Trelissick Park Loop walk is that it provides an easy way for hikers to get away from the hustle and bustle of the city while still being in the nature reserve close to the center of Wellington. A walk through this trail is perfect for hikers that want to unwind and be amazed by nature’s beauty. The shady forest walk, the stream, and the native birdsong create the ultimate stress-relief experience. Another advantage of this trail is that it has a variety of paths, offering walkers an interesting trail that will keep them engaged without tiring them due to the mixture of inclines and declines along the way. Similar toŌtari-Wilton’s Bush, Trelissick Park Loop also has lots of birdlife and offers hikers an opportunity to experience a walk in the forest where they can hear and possibly see native birds. Trelissick Park Loop is ideally suited for people who enjoy shorter and easier walks in the forest with spectacular views.
