Porirua and Tawa

Te Ngahere O Tawa

Te Ngahere-o-Tawa is a beautiful bush walk set in the hills of Tawa. It's a peaceful walk through the wilderness bursting with birdsong and offering postcard views of the suburbs and coastline. You should do this track because it's a lovely nature getaway where you can enjoy the scenery, get some much-needed exercise, and experience a unique wilderness walk without having to go far from the city. The track is a fairly gentle climb and features both native bush and pine tree forest giving it a more variety than your average trail walk. Te Ngahere-o-Tawa is a peaceful bush walk that will have you meandering past native birds and towering pine trees. One of the best things about Te Ngahere-o-Tawa is that it has a real wilderness feel to it, despite being only a short drive from the city. The trail has a nice mix of native bush and pine tree forest. This means you'll have a nice variety of scenery to enjoy on your walk. The track is a fairly easy climb so it won't be too strenuous, but it still has enough bite to give you a good workout. The views over Tawa are also something special. Another thing worth mentioning about this track is that it's a great way to enjoy a nature getaway without having to venture too far from the city. Te Ngahere-o-Tawa is a great choice if you're looking for a relatively short walk that will still give you a good workout, and a chance to enjoy some beautiful scenery.

Redwood Bush

Redwood Bush is a lovely loop walk through one of Wellington's finest native bush reserves. This trail is a perfect excuse to get out and about, enjoy the natural beauty of New Zealand, and have a walk in the bush. You should do this track because it's a peaceful bush experience, it's the ultimate way to unwind and reconnect with nature, and it offers walkers the ultimate chance to escape the hustle and bustle of everyday life. Redwood Bush is a beautiful walk track that's the perfect excuse to get out and about and enjoy the natural beauty of New Zealand. The track winds through towering trees and lush green foliage giving you wonderful insight into New Zealand's unique native bush. One of the best things about Redwood Bush Loop Track is that it's a fairly straightforward walk through some of New Zealand's finest scenery. The trail is a wonderful example of New Zealand's native forest and is bursting with natural beauty. Because Redwood Bush is set in such a picturesque location, it's the perfect excuse for walkers to unwind and de-stress. Another reason why this walk is so special is that it's super shady and pretty. The trail makes the most of its forested surroundings and offers walkers a pleasant, sheltered walk. This track is a must-do for nature lovers and is the perfect family-friendly track if you're looking for a nice walk in the bush. The peaceful surroundings make it the ultimate way to unwind and disconnect from the stresses of everyday life.

Mount Rangituhi

The Rangituhi walk is a scenic and satisfying bush walk track located in Porirua. It's a fairly gentle amble through the forest with rewarding views over distant harbours, the coast, and suburban sprawls. You should do this track because it's a lovely nature getaway where you can enjoy the scenery, get some much-needed exercise, and experience a unique wilderness walk without having to go far from the city. The trail boasts spectacular views over Porirua, but what sets it apart from other local walks is that it has rewarding 360-degree views to the surrounding hills and valley. The Rangituhi walk is a scenic and satisfying bush walk track located on the edge of Porirua. It's a fairly gentle amble through the forest with rewarding views over distant harbours, the coast, and suburban sprawls. One of the reasons why you should do this track is that it's a lovely nature getaway where you can enjoy the scenery, get some much-needed exercise, and experience a unique wilderness walk without having to go far from the city. Rangituhi offers spectacular views over the harbour, but what sets it apart from other local walks in the area is that it has rewarding 360-degree views to the surrounding hills and valley. Another thing that makes Rangituhi special is that it's a fairly straightforward track that won't leave you gasping for breath after the first few minutes. The trail offers walkers a nice mix of bush and open space, as it snakes its way up and over the hill. The steady climb up the hill is a real test, but it's made all the more satisfying by the spectacular views that open up before you once you reach the top. On a clear day, you'll see panoramic views over Porirua Harbour, the coast, and the surrounding city. Mt Rangituhi is the perfect track for walkers looking for a more substantial bush walk that will test their fitness, but won't leave them absolutely wrecked at the end of it.
