Wellington's Walks

Central Wellington

Skyline Walkway

The Skyline Walkway in Wellington is one of the city’s most scenic and adventurous tracks, stretching across the hills with incredible panoramic views of the harbour, coastline, and surrounding countryside. The walk is popular with hikers, runners, and mountain bikers because of its open landscapes and peaceful atmosphere. Along the track, walkers can enjoy fresh air, rolling hills, and native plants while getting a great workout from the steep sections. The Skyline Walkway is a fun way to experience Wellington’s natural beauty and is especially rewarding on clear days when the views stretch for miles.

Southern Walkway

The Southern Walkway is a well-known walking track in Wellington that takes walkers through a mix of city streets, parks, forest areas, and hilltops. The track connects several parts of the city and offers amazing views of the harbour, Cook Strait, and nearby suburbs. Many people enjoy this walk because it combines exercise with sightseeing, making it both relaxing and exciting. The Southern Walkway is also a great way to explore Wellington’s history and environment, with plenty of places to stop, rest, and take in the scenery along the journey.

Red Rocks Walk

The Red Rocks Walk, located on Wellington’s rugged south coast, is famous for its dramatic coastal scenery and unique red-coloured rocks. This walk is popular with both locals and tourists because it offers stunning ocean views, fresh sea air, and the chance to see seals resting near the shoreline during certain times of the year. The wide gravel path makes the walk enjoyable for many fitness levels, while the crashing waves and rocky landscape create an exciting atmosphere. Walking at Red Rocks is both relaxing and refreshing, giving visitors the opportunity to connect with nature and enjoy one of Wellington’s most beautiful coastal areas.
