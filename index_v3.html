Wellington's Walks

Local walks in Wellington

Wellington is one of New Zealand’s best cities for walking, offering a mix of scenic coastal paths, bush tracks, and city walks that suit people of all ages and fitness levels. From the waterfront promenade to the peaceful trails of the hills surrounding the city, walks in Wellington provide both enjoyment and important health benefits. Walking regularly can improve physical fitness, strengthen the heart, reduce stress, and boost mental wellbeing, while also giving people the chance to connect with nature and explore the beauty of the region. Many popular walks, such as those around the harbour or through native bush reserves, offer stunning views and fresh air that make exercise feel relaxing rather than difficult. Whether someone is looking for a short stroll or a more challenging hike, Wellington’s variety of walking tracks makes it easy to stay active while enjoying the outdoors.
