Wellington's Walks

Johnsonville and Kandallah

Mount Kaukau

The Mount Kaukau walk is one of Wellington’s most popular hikes thanks to its incredible panoramic views and accessible tracks. Rising above Khandallah and Johnsonville, the walk takes you through open hillsides and well-formed paths before reaching the summit lookout beside the iconic transmission tower. From the top, walkers are rewarded with sweeping views of Wellington Harbour, the Hutt Valley, Porirua, and even the South Island on a clear day. It’s a great walk for both fitness and sightseeing, with plenty of fresh air and a real sense of achievement once you reach the summit.

Otari Wilton Bush Reserve

Ōtari-Wilton’s Bush is a peaceful native forest reserve and New Zealand’s only public botanic garden dedicated entirely to native plants. The reserve offers a variety of tracks ranging from short easy strolls to longer bush walks through towering rimu trees, lush ferns, and tranquil streams. One of the highlights is the canopy walkway and the chance to see native birds such as tūī, kererū, and kākā in their natural habitat. This walk is perfect for people wanting to escape the city and experience Wellington’s natural beauty while learning about New Zealand’s unique native environment and conservation efforts.

Trelissick Park loop

The Trelissick Park Loop is a scenic bush walk that follows winding tracks through regenerating native forest alongside the Kaiwharawhara Stream. The loop combines peaceful greenery, birdlife, and gentle stream crossings, making it feel surprisingly wild despite being close to the city. The walk is especially popular with locals because of its relaxed atmosphere and variety of tracks that can suit both casual walkers and regular runners. It’s an excellent choice for anyone looking for a quieter nature walk where they can enjoy birdsong, shaded trails, and a calm escape from busy urban life.
